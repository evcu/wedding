Getting here

Ayvalık & the nearest airports

Ayvalık sits on Turkey's northern Aegean coast. There's no airport in town — most guests will fly into one of these and drive the rest of the way. Hover or tap a pin for driving time; the table below has the full detail.

Airport detail
Airport Distance Driving time Notes
Balıkesir Koca Seyit
EDO · Edremit		 47 km ~45 min Closest by far. Mostly domestic routes via Istanbul.
Izmir Adnan Menderes
ADB		 140 km ~2–2.5 hr More international options; a good middle ground.
Sabiha Gökçen
SAW · Istanbul (Asian side)		 ~410 km ~4 hr Stays on the Asian mainland the whole way — no strait crossing, so it beats flying into IST despite being further from the city center.
Istanbul
IST · European side		 ~440 km ~4 hr 45 min The biggest hub and best long-haul connections, but the drive crosses the Dardanelles via the 1915 Çanakkale Bridge — a connecting flight into EDO or ADB is often faster door to door.
